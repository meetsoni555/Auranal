# Auranal - Journaling with Aura , in the terminal.

 A dynamic command-line journaling tool designed to capture the nuance of your day through categorized entries and personalized mood tracking in a gamified way .

## How it works
- Categorization: Choose from preset categories or create a custom category to organize your thoughts (e.g., Work, Personal, Deep Work, or Ideas).
- The Journal: Write your entry directly through the CLI.
- Mood Tracking: After writing, Auranal prompts you to log your mood. Use standard presets or define your own custom moods to accurately reflect your headspace.
- The Vault: Everything is saved locally as .txt files within a dedicated vault/ folder, ensuring your data is portable, readable, and private.

  


## Keyfeatures 
- Custom Taxonomy: Don't be boxed in—create categories that fit your specific life.
- Granular Moods: Go beyond "Happy" or "Sad" with user-defined mood states.
- Local-First: Your data is stored in plain text files within your personal vault.
- Minimalist Workflow: Designed for speed and focus, keeping you in the flow of your terminal.
